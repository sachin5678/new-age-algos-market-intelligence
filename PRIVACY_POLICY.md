# Privacy Policy — New Age Algos

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

New Age Algos ("the app") is a personal automation tool that produces and
publishes videos for the New Age Algos YouTube channel. This policy describes
what it accesses and stores.

## What the app accesses

- **YouTube account** — only to upload videos to the owner's own channel, via
  the YouTube Data API, using the access granted during Google sign-in
  (scope: upload videos). It does not read, modify or delete other content.
- **Public market data** — stock quotes, news headlines and RSS feeds, all
  publicly available, used to script and narrate the videos.

## What is stored

- OAuth credentials (client id, client secret and a refresh token), kept on
  the owner's machine and in GitHub Actions secrets — never shared or sold.
- An upload registry (which video was published when) so the same story is
  never published twice.

## What is NOT collected

- No end-user personal data, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies. Viewers of
  the YouTube channel are not tracked by this app in any way.

## Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising. The only third party that
receives anything is Google (YouTube), solely as required to upload the videos.
